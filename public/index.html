Frequently asked

How does the offline mode work? +
Our ESP32 hardware caches all fingerprint scans locally on an SD card if the internet goes down. Once the connection is restored, it automatically syncs the backlog to the cloud.
Can it integrate with our existing database? +
Yes. Because our backend uses PostgreSQL (Neon) and a REST API, we can easily export your attendance logs to CSV, or build a direct webhook integration with your school's student management system.
Is the hardware included? +
Yes. When you partner with SentinEx, we provide the full package: the biometric terminal (built with the AS608 sensor), the power supply, the 3D-printed enclosure, and the cloud dashboard setup.
How long does installation take? +
A standard deployment for a single office or classroom takes less than 2 hours. For a full campus (multiple buildings), we usually complete installation and staff training within 2 days.
How secure is the biometric data? +
Extremely secure. The AS608 sensor does not store actual fingerprint images. It converts your fingerprint into a mathematical template (a hash) and stores only that. This template cannot be reverse-engineered into a picture of your finger.
