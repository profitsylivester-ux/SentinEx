SentinEx System Manual

1. Injured Student Workflow

  • If a student cannot scan their finger, go to the Members Directory.
  • Find the student's name and click the "Mark Present" button.
  • The system will log them as "Manual" for today's date.

2. Managing Members

  • Add: Click "+ Add New Member" to assign a Fingerprint ID and Reg No.
  • Edit/Delete: Update details or move students to the Trash Bin safely.
