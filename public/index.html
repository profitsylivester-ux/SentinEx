SentinEx System Manual

1. How the Hardware Works

  • Normal Scan: A student places their finger on the sensor. The device beeps and the LCD shows "Welcome". The data is instantly sent to this dashboard.
  • Injured Finger (Manual Entry): If a student's finger cannot be read, they must press the * (star) key on the device keypad, type their Assigned ID Number (e.g., 3), and press # to submit.
  • Power Saving: To turn off the device, press and hold the physical power button for 1 second. To wake it up, press it once.

2. How the Dashboard Works

  • Date Filter: Use the calendar in the top right to view attendance for a specific day. It defaults to "Today".
  • Security Alerts: If an unknown finger tries to scan, it is flagged as "Denied" here.
  • Duplicate Scans: If a student scans more than once a day, a ⚠️ warning icon will appear next to their name.

3. Managing Students (Members)

  • Go to the Members Directory tab to see everyone registered in the system.
  • Click the Delete button to remove a student. They will be moved to the Trash Bin.
  • If you deleted someone by mistake, go to the Trash Bin and click Restore.

4. Troubleshooting

  • The dashboard says "Live Connection" but no data appears? Ensure the device is plugged in and the phone hotspot is turned on.
  • Device screen is blank? Press the power button once to wake it up. If it doesn't wake, ensure the USB power cable is connected.
