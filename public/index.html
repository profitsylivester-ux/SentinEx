Frequently asked

How does the offline mode work?
Our ESP32 hardware caches all fingerprint scans locally on an SD card if the internet goes down. Once restored, it syncs to the cloud.
Can it integrate with our existing database?
Yes. Because our backend uses PostgreSQL and a REST API, we can easily export logs to CSV.
Is the hardware included?
Yes. When you partner with SentinEx, we provide the full package: the terminal, power supply, enclosure, and cloud setup.
How long does installation take?
A standard deployment for a single office or classroom takes less than 2 hours. A full campus takes 2 days.
How secure is the biometric data?
Extremely secure. The AS608 sensor does not store images. It converts your fingerprint into a mathematical template (hash).

SentinEx System Manual

1. Hardware Overview

  • Normal Scan: Place your finger on the sensor. The device beeps and the LCD shows your name. The data is instantly synced to this cloud dashboard.
  • Injured Finger: If your finger cannot be read, press the * (star) key on the keypad. Type your Assigned ID (e.g., 3) and press #. This sends a Pending Request to the Admin.
  • Power Saving: The system runs on a schedule. It shuts down at Closing Time and wakes up at Start Time automatically. You can also press and hold the physical power button for 1 second to sleep it manually.

2. Syncing Offline Data (SD Card)

  • If the internet goes down, the device saves all scans to the built-in SD card.
  • When the internet is restored, take the SD card out, plug it into your PC, and open the attendance.csv file.
  • Click the Upload Offline CSV button on this dashboard and select the file. The system will automatically parse the data and save it to the cloud.

3. Managing Members

  • Add Member: Go to the Members Directory and click '+ Add New Member'. Enter their Full Name, Registration Number, and Fingerprint ID slot (1-150).
  • Edit/Delete: Click 'Edit' to fix typos. Click 'Delete' to move a student to the Trash Bin. You can restore them from the Trash Bin if needed.

4. Attendance & Time Settings

  • Go to the Settings tab to configure your school's schedule.
  • Start Time: Scans before this time will be denied.
  • Late Time: Scans after this time will be marked as 'Late' (Red badge).
  • Closing Time: Scans after this time will be denied.

5. Audit Logs

  • Every action you take (Adding students, changing settings, deleting records) is permanently recorded here. This ensures transparency and prevents internal fraud.
